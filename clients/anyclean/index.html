07 · Google Ads management

Running campaigns is one thing.
Managing them actively is another.

The goal is not simply cheaper clicks. It is more completed work for the same, or better controlled, acquisition budget.

Common self-managed risks to check
01Search terms that look relevant but never book
02Budget spread across the wrong cities or services
03Automated bidding trained on every form, not real jobs
04Leads lost between click, call, quote and booking
What a dedicated operator should deliver
01Weekly search term pruning and negative keyword work
02Service-by-city budget and creative experiments
03Offline booked-job conversion feedback into Ads
04Clear scorecards linking spend to completed revenue
Optimization practices to evaluate, not claims about the current Anyclean account8 / 13

09 · A second compounding acquisition channel

Build organic demand alongside
paid search performance.

Search engine optimization and AI search visibility should reduce dependence on paid acquisition over time, not be sold as a magic way to lower Google auction CPC.

Search engine optimization · SEO

Win more relevant unpaid visits.

Improve technical performance, local service-page uniqueness, internal links, genuinely useful service content, reviews and city coverage. Measure organic enquiries and completed jobs, not rankings alone.

Opportunity for Anyclean

460 published area pages create both an opportunity for coverage and a need to evaluate page quality and differentiation.

AI and answer engine visibility · AEO / GEO

Be discoverable where answers are formed.

Develop trustworthy, well-structured service information, local evidence and authoritative mentions. Track referral visibility, branded searches and qualified visits where measurable.

Google says good SEO fundamentals also apply to AI Overviews and AI Mode. No special AI-only markup or guaranteed placement exists.

Sources. Anyclean locations. Google Search Central, AI features and your website.10 / 13
