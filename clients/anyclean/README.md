# Anyclean Growth Strategy, Ampgent × Crowdset

Client-facing browser presentation, prepared November 4, 2026.

## Use

Open `index.html` in a browser. Use the left and right arrow keys to navigate, F for fullscreen, P for print. Sliders on slides 4, 7 and 11 recalculate hypothetical business scenarios live.

## Source boundaries

* CAD 1.2 million annual sales was reported in the initial owner prospect conversation and has not been independently verified. The presentation models *gross completed-job billings* rather than asserting that the company recognizes all revenue.
* 5,684 cumulative jobs, 460 area pages, historical city counts, category shares, and combined public review score are Anyclean's own claims, refreshed 2026-10-18. https://anyclean.ca/locations/
* The 2025 LocaliQ search-ad benchmark for cleaning and maid services, $8.50 average CPC, 17.65% lead CVR, $46.99 CPL, is from the US and denominated in USD. It is **not** Canadian Keyword Planner or Anyclean account data. https://localiq.com/blog/home-services-search-advertising-benchmarks/
* Illustrative CAD $4 CPC, 8% CVR, $50 CPL, the 8% to 10% to 12% improvement examples, and SEO organic lead scenarios are arithmetic sensitivities only, with no promised lift or verified keyword-level Canadian bids.
* Google Search Central notes foundational SEO practices also apply to AI Overviews and AI Mode, with no special AI-only technical requirements. https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features
* Crowdset public website verifies PPC, SEO, digital consulting and lead-nurturing services. It did **not** expose independently verifiable named testimonials in the accessible content, so no testimonial was invented. https://www.crowdset.cc/

## Data and access needed to build a genuine business case

Read-only Google Ads, Google Analytics 4, Google Search Console, Google Tag Manager, call-tracking and booking/CRM data, service-unit economics, repeat rate, crew capacity, real qualified-lead definition, true customer booking attribution, and actual Keyword Planner city/keyword volume and bid data. Never collect passwords or private keys from the prospect.

## Deployment safety

This presentation contains the prospect-reported annual revenue. If deployed on GitHub Pages, the site may be publicly accessible even when the repository is private. Use a separate orphan `gh-pages` branch with only presentation-safe material. Avoid exposing source repository contents or secrets.
