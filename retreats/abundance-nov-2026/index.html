01 / The intention

Not a packed program. Not another party. A warm, unhurried weekend that leaves room for the things we remember most.

Daryna's wellness vision is the heart of the weekend: gentle movement, meditation with vision notes, nourishing meals and genuine time to connect. Our addition is simple: let's step outside the house for fresh air, a little discovery and one quintessential County experience.

Important for our group

Dining out is conditional, not booked.

Both venues are confirmed participants in Countylicious, which runs through November 15. Neither has confirmed a suitable table for approximately 15 people on November 14. The Royal requests that parties above 10 book through its events team. Drake Devonshire requires groups of 13 or more to book through Special Events; parties of 8 or more receive a single bill and a 20% gratuity. Confirm whether the Countylicious offer applies to group bookings before making plans. If we cannot arrange an appropriate meal at The Royal or Drake Devonshire, we stay at the house.

For anyone who wants a separate Sunday excursion, the GIFTED Holiday Market at ANDARA Gallery in Bloomfield runs November 13 to 15, from 11 AM to 7 PM. This is optional and not part of the group schedule.
