01 / The intention

Not a packed program. Not another party. A warm, unhurried weekend that leaves room for the things we remember most.

Daryna's wellness vision is the heart of the weekend: gentle movement, meditation with vision notes, nourishing meals and genuine time to connect. Our addition is simple: choose a relaxed route outdoors, then decide together whether sunset is better at Drake or back at the house.

SIMPLEST EVENING · NO BOOKING REQUIRED AT HOME
Important for a group of around fifteen

Confirm the visit before we commit.

Drake's restaurant publishes daily happy hour from 3 PM to 5 PM and dinner from 5 PM to 9 PM. The patio's summer marketing does not guarantee outdoor seating in mid November. For indoor seating, a snack stop or a Countylicious dinner with this group, contact their special events team first. Drake notes that parties of 13 or more require special events booking, and groups of eight or more receive one bill and a 20 percent gratuity.

Wellington Rotary Beach is an excellent public waterfront fallback for the view if Drake cannot accommodate the group. On the Picton side, the Royal remains another high quality dinner option only if group interest and a suitable reservation justify it.

08 / A note for the hosts

Keep the heart.
Add the adventure.

This is an evolution of the wellness plan, not a replacement. The short yoga practice, notebook meditation and personal growth pieces give the weekend meaning; a single County outing, the choice of Drake at sunset or sunset at home, and entirely optional dining make it distinctly Prince Edward County in November.

Let's decide together what feels right for the people coming. This lookbook is a set of options to discuss — not a public event ticket or a finalized itinerary.
